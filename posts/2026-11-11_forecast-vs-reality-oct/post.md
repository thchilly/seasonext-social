## LinkedIn

Forecast vs reality, October 2026: did the seasonal forecasts get it right?

Every month we look back at what the seasonal forecasts said for Crete about a month ahead, and compare it with what actually happened. Hits and misses alike.

Rainfall. At the beginning of September, the forecasts gave a 53% chance of a wetter than normal October (with no information at all, the chance would be 33%). It was wet: 167 mm over Crete in ERA5, more than three times the October average of 1993-2016 (49 mm), and the second wettest October of 1993-2026 after 2006. The month began with the storm that hit the island from 30 September to 4 October. A hit.

Temperature. The forecasts leaned clearly towards a warm October (70% chance). It turned out cool: 17.1 °C, about 2 °C below the 1993-2016 average, and one of the two coolest Octobers of 1993-2026. A clear miss.

A 70% chance is not a promise. If the forecasts are reliable, something given a 70% chance should fail to happen about three times in ten. That is why we keep score month after month: only a long record shows how far these odds can be trusted. Turning seasonal forecasts into information that water managers in Crete can act on is what SEASONEXT is about.

How to read the figure: "normal" is the middle third of the Octobers of 1993-2016 (grey dots). The forecast is the average of 10 Copernicus (C3S) seasonal forecast systems; the observations come from ERA5, a reanalysis that combines weather observations with a weather model.

Data: C3S seasonal forecasts and ERA5. Contains modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information 2026.

## X and Bluesky

How did the seasonal forecasts do for Crete in October? Rain: they leaned wet (53%), and it was the second wettest October of 1993-2026 in ERA5. Temperature: they leaned warm (70%), and it came in cool, about 2 °C below normal. One hit, one miss.

## Alt text

Graphic titled "Did the forecasts get October right?", two panels for October 2026 in Crete. Rainfall: forecast chances of 19% drier, 28% near normal and 53% wetter than normal; the observed 167 mm is in the wet third, well above its lower edge of 61 mm. Temperature: forecast chances of 9% cooler, 20% near normal and 70% warmer than normal; the observed 17.1 degrees C is in the cool third, well below its upper edge of 18.9 degrees C.

## Sources and checks

- Forecast: C3S seasonal-monthly-single-levels, start September 2026, lead month 2 (October). 10 systems: ECMWF 51, UKMO 610, Meteo-France 9, DWD 22, CMCC 4, NCEP 2, JMA 4, ECCC 4 and 5, BoM 2. Tercile probabilities per system against its own 1993-2016 hindcast, averaged with equal weights. Multi-system: rainfall 19 / 28 / 53%, temperature 9 / 20 / 70% (rounded; 9.3 + 20.2 + 70.4).
- Systems leaning: rainfall 9 of 10 leaned wet (CMCC 34% each, no signal); temperature all 10 leaned warm (51% to 85%).
- Observed: ERA5 monthly means, Crete land points, terciles from 1993-2016.
- Rainfall: observed 167.3 mm; thirds split at 27.9 and 60.7 mm; 1993-2016 mean 49.2 mm (167.3 / 49.2 = 3.4). Highest in 1993-2026: 171.2 mm (2006), so October 2026 is second. Clear of the line: a hit.
- Temperature: observed 17.13 °C; thirds split at 18.88 and 19.71 °C; 1993-2016 mean 19.19 °C (difference 2.06 °C). Lowest in 1993-2026: 17.09 °C (2011), so October 2026 is second lowest, within 0.04 °C; hence "one of the two coolest". Clear of the line: a miss.
- Storm dates (30 September to 4 October) as in the post "Crete floods in context" (8 Oct).
