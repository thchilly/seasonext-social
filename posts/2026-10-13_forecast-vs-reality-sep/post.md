## LinkedIn

New monthly series, forecast vs reality: did the seasonal forecasts get it right?

Every month we will look back at what the seasonal forecasts said for Crete about a month ahead, and compare it with what actually happened. Hits and misses alike.

September 2026:

Rainfall. At the beginning of August, the forecasts gave a 49% chance of a wetter than normal September (with no information at all, the chance would be 33%). It was wet: 78 mm over Crete in ERA5, the wettest September of 1993-2026, helped by the first day of the storm that hit the island on September 30.

Temperature. The forecasts were confident in a warm September (76% chance). It came in right on the line between cool and near normal. A miss.

Why show the misses? A 76% chance is not a promise. If the forecasts are reliable, something given a 76% chance should fail to happen about one time in four. Only by keeping score, month after month, can users learn how far to trust them. Building that trust, and turning seasonal forecasts into information water managers in Crete can act on, is what SEASONEXT is about.

How to read the figure: "normal" is the middle third of the Septembers of 1993-2016 (grey dots). The forecast is the average of 10 Copernicus (C3S) seasonal forecast systems; the observations come from ERA5.

SEASONEXT is led by Hydromech, the Hydrology and Hydraulic Engineering Laboratory, TUC.

## X and Bluesky

How did the seasonal forecasts do for Crete in September? Rain: they leaned wet (49%), and it was the wettest September of 1993-2026 in ERA5. Temperature: they leaned warm (76%), it came in cool to normal. One hit, one miss. A new monthly series.

## Alt text

Graphic titled "Did the forecasts get September right?", two panels for September 2026 in Crete. Rainfall: forecast chances of 19% drier, 32% near normal and 49% wetter than normal; the observed 78 mm is the wettest September of 1993-2026, in the wet third. Temperature: forecast chances of 5% cooler, 18% near normal and 76% warmer than normal; the observed 22.7 degrees C sits on the line between the cool and normal thirds.

## Sources and checks

- Forecast: C3S seasonal-monthly-single-levels, start August 2026, lead month 2 (September). 10 systems: ECMWF 51, UKMO 610, Meteo-France 9, DWD 22, CMCC 4, NCEP 2, JMA 4, ECCC 4 and 5, BoM 2. Tercile probabilities per system against its own 1993-2016 hindcast, averaged with equal weights.
- Observed: ERA5 monthly means, Crete land points (11 cells), terciles from 1993-2016.
- Temperature: observed 22.67 degrees C, cool-third edge 22.68 degrees C, hence "on the line".
- Rainfall: 77.6 mm vs previous highest 71.3 mm (2002) in the 1993-2026 ERA5 series.
