# SEASONEXT brand

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `logo/seasonext_logo.png` | Logo with tagline; figure footers |
| `logo/seasonext_emblem.png` | Arrow emblem; avatars and small spaces |
| `fonts/SourceSerif4-*.ttf` | Source Serif 4 (Adobe), titles and headline numbers (SIL Open Font License, `fonts/SourceSerif4-LICENSE.md`) |
| `fonts/Barlow-*.ttf` | Barlow, a DIN-style typeface close to the logo lettering; labels, data and credits (SIL Open Font License, `fonts/Barlow-OFL.txt`) |

Colours (from the logo): navy `#151d2c`, blue `#4d71b1`, blue tints `#7994c4`, `#a5b6d7`,
`#d0d9e9`. Figures add terracotta `#c0703f` as the opposite pole to blue (dry or warm)
and greys for secondary text and hairlines. Figures sit on warm off-white paper `#faf8f3`.

Taglines: "Seasonal hydrological outlooks for water extremes"; "Look forward, plan backwards".
